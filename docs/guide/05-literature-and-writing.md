# Literature and writing

Your own inputs (interviews, discussions) go to `docs/inputs/`. Zotero papers become snapshots in `docs/literature-review/papers/` with their colour-coded annotations. In explore, three playbooks run in order. Proposal intake comes first: an interview saved verbatim in `docs/rstack/explore/00_interview.md`, then context, aims, milestones, and a task list written for your project. Literature review then runs the Zotero loop and records each paper in `docs/literature-review/references.yaml`. Proposal synthesis ends explore with questions, methods, and an `interrogate` check. Notes go to `docs/literature-review/`. Every citation is resolved before it enters a draft.

Writing, reports and slides belong to the wstack plugin (`docs/wstack`, `docs/reports`, `docs/writing`, `docs/presentation`). rstack supplies `analysis/` and `docs/results/`. Use Peer review on drafts and Reviewer response for comments.
