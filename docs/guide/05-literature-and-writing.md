# Literature and writing

Your own inputs (interviews, discussions) go to `docs/inputs/`. Zotero papers become snapshots in `docs/literature-review/papers/` with their colour-coded annotations. In explore, three playbooks run in order. Proposal intake comes first: an interview saved verbatim in `docs/inputs/<date>_interview_<slug>.md`, then context, aims, milestones, and a task list written for your project. Literature review then runs the Zotero loop and records each paper in `docs/literature-review/references.yaml`. Report init ends explore with questions, methods, and an `interrogate` check. Notes go to `docs/literature-review/`. Every citation is resolved before it enters a draft.

Writing, reports and slides are the write phase. The control files live in `docs/rstack/writing/<piece>/`, one folder per question in `docs/reports/<question>/`, your text in `docs/writing/<piece>/` and `docs/presentation/<piece>/`. You write every paragraph; the assistant prepares the report from `analysis/` and `docs/results/`, asks questions, and critiques. Use Peer review on drafts and Reviewer response for comments.
