# Literature and writing

Your own inputs (interviews, discussions) go to `docs/raw/inputs/`. Zotero papers become snapshots in `docs/raw/papers/` with their colour-coded annotations. In explore, a scope interview comes first and rewrites the task list for your project. Notes go to `docs/wiki/`, maintained with `karpathy-llm-wiki`. Every citation is resolved before it enters a draft.

Manuscripts live in `manuscripts/`. Numbers are copied from `analysis/` files. Use Peer review on drafts, Reviewer response for comments, and Presentation for slides and posters in `presentation/`.
