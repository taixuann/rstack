# Route work through /research-mode

`/research-mode` restates the research question and the decision it supports, matches one playbook from `skills/research-mode/playbooks/`, copies its steps into the todo list, and runs the skills the steps need.

Write the goal and the check, not the steps. "Repro first" and "every number traced" are the routing signals.

When no playbook fits, the mode uses `/figure-it-out` to design one for the task.
