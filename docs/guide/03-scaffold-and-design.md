# Scaffold and design

**Init.** `/rstack:init` creates `data/raw`, `docs/raw`, `docs/wiki`, `analysis`, `manuscripts`, `presentation`. `data/raw` is immutable.

**Design.** The Experimental design playbook writes a note at `docs/rstack/design/<date>_<name>.md`: one question, falsifiable hypothesis, primary endpoint, design with tradeoffs, sample size with its inputs, and the pre-stated analysis plan. Use `/arena` when two designs are plausible and `/interrogate` to attack the final one.
