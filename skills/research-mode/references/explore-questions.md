# Explore question bank

General-purpose questions for the `proposal` playbook. Ask in short rounds of one to four related questions, recommended option first, and offer "you suggest" when the user is unsure. Skip what the user already answered. Save every answer verbatim in `docs/rstack/explore/00_interview.md`. Never invent an answer.

**Context and goal**
- What is the project in one or two sentences, and who is it for (thesis, paper, prototype, grant)?
- What does the supervisor or funder expect at the end? What would count as success?
- Why now? What problem or opportunity started this?

**Scope and work packages**
- What will you actually do, start to finish? Which parts are separate enough to be their own work package?
- What is out of scope?
- Which parts depend on each other?

**Stack and methods**
- Which domains are involved (for example materials and electrochemistry, fabrication, characterization, embedded hardware and firmware, signal processing, machine learning, clinical or human-subject work)?
- Which methods, instruments, or tools do you already plan to use?
- What do you already know well, and what do you need to learn?

**Milestones and time**
- What is the total duration and the hard deadlines?
- What are the milestones, and what is each deliverable?
- What is the riskiest or slowest step?

**Resources and constraints**
- Which equipment, lab access, software, and budget do you have?
- What data exists already, and what must be collected?
- Which ethics, consent, safety, or standards requirements apply?

**What is already known**
- Which papers, reviews, or standards do you already know are central? Are they in Zotero (which collection)?
- Do you want me to search, or will you supply the papers?
- Which claims in the field do you doubt or find unclear?

**Risks and feasibility**
- What could stop this working, and what is the fallback?
- What would make you change the plan?
