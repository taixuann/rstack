# Plain writing rules

Use these rules for lesson text, explanations and comment replies. They follow the main ideas of Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100), written here in our own words. They make text easy to read for a person who reads English as a second language.

## Rules

1. **Short sentences.** Use 20 words or fewer for a step or an instruction. Use 25 words or fewer for an explanation. One idea in each sentence.
2. **One word, one meaning.** Use the same word each time for the same thing. Do not swap "lead", "channel" and "signal" for variety.
3. **Active voice and present tense.** Write "The amplifier subtracts B from A". Do not write "B is subtracted from A" or "B will be subtracted".
4. **Start a step with a verb.** Write "Add Lead I and Lead III". Do not write "You might want to add".
5. **Put the condition first.** Write "If the electrodes are 1 cm apart, the line is flat".
6. **Plain verbs, no idioms.** Do not use phrases such as "boils down to" or "sits at". Do not use contractions.
7. **Name the thing.** Repeat the noun when "it" or "they" could point at two things.
8. **No more than three nouns in a row.** Write "the skin contact impedance", not "the electrode skin contact impedance value".
9. **Numbers and units.** Replace "very small" or "fast" with a number and a unit when you have one. If you have none, say so or leave the word out.
10. **Define a term in the sentence where it first appears.**
11. **Structure only when it helps.** Use a small heading to split a long explanation by topic. Use a list for steps or parallel items. Use a table only for three or more things that you compare on the same points. Otherwise write short paragraphs.

## Exceptions

- A quote from the user or a paper stays exactly as written.
- An official name or a symbol stays as written.
- The lesson language follows the user. Write in the language they ask for, and apply the same rules.

## Check

Read the text once before you send it. Split any sentence that is longer than 25 words. Replace any word that has two possible meanings.
