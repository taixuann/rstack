### Pause safely

Suspend in-flight work so it can resume (going offline, a long break, context nearly full).

1. Finish or cleanly abandon the current unit. Do not leave a half-written output next to good ones.
2. Save scripts, outputs, and the decision log. Write `docs/rstack/PAUSE.md`: state, next three steps, open questions, commands to rerun.
3. Report where everything is.

**Reply:** the pause note path and the first step on resume.
