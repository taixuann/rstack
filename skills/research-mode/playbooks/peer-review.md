### Peer review

Review someone else's manuscript, proposal, or analysis.

1. Read the whole artifact before judging. Note the claims and the evidence offered for each.
2. Use the `peer-review` skill. Check methods against claims, statistics against design, figures against numbers.
3. Report BLOCKING issues separately from observations and suggestions, with a calibrated recommendation.
4. Do not rewrite the work; point to the location and the fix needed.

**Reply:** summary of the work, blocking issues, other concerns, and recommendation.
