### Reviewer response

Answer reviewer comments on a submitted manuscript.

1. List every comment in a table: id, reviewer, concern, action (change, clarify, disagree).
2. For each change, make it in `manuscripts/` and quote the new text with its location. Rerun analyses in `analysis/` when a comment needs new numbers.
3. Disagree only with evidence, politely and specifically.
4. `peer-review` on your own revised draft as a final check. Verify no number in the letter differs from the manuscript.

**Reply:** point-by-point letter and a summary of changes, plus any comment you could not address.
