# Manuscript writing

State: ready-for-publish. Output: drafts in `manuscripts/`.

1. Confirm journal, section, and the one-sentence key message.
2. Judge and reshape the story from the figures before writing prose.
3. `scientific-writing` (from `claude-scientific-writer`) to draft and revise. Claims trace to `analysis/` outputs or verified references; numbers are copied from files.
4. `scientific-schematics` for method or concept diagrams and `citation-management` for the bibliography (both from `claude-scientific-writer`). `venue-templates` for journal formats.
5. Check consistency: sample sizes, endpoints, and statistics identical in abstract, results, and tables.
6. `peer-review` to review a draft; `interrogate` to stress-test the argument. For reviewer responses, use the Reviewer response playbook.
7. Final prose pass: cut filler and stacked hedges.
