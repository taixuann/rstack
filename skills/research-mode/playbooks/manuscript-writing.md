# Manuscript writing

Output: drafts in `manuscripts/`.

1. Confirm journal, section, and the one-sentence key message. `venue-templates` for format and limits.
2. `paper-narrative` first when figures exist: judge and reshape the story before writing prose.
3. `scientific-writing` to draft, revise, and audit. Claims trace to `analysis/` outputs or verified references; numbers are copied from files.
4. `scientific-schematics` for method or concept diagrams.
5. Check consistency: sample sizes, endpoints, statistics identical in abstract, results, and tables.
6. `peer-review` to review a draft; `interrogate` to stress-test the argument. For reviewer responses, answer point by point, quote the change made, and disagree with evidence where warranted.
7. Final prose pass: cut filler and stacked hedges.
