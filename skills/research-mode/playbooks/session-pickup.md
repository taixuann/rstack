### Session pickup

Resume or take over in-flight work from notes, a transcript, or a decision log.

1. Read the latest plan, decision log, and `docs/rstack/progressing/results.md`. Treat their claims as unverified.
2. Check the real state: files present, scripts rerunnable, last outputs matching the log.
3. Write what is done, what is open, and what is uncertain. Continue under the matching playbook.

**Reply:** the verified state and the next step.
