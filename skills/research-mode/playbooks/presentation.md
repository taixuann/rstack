# Presentation

State: ready-for-publish. Output: deck or poster in `presentation/`, plus speaker notes.

1. Establish audience, time slot, goal (inform, persuade, defend). About one slide per minute.
2. Outline as a storyline of full-sentence claims; each slide carries one figure or one idea.
3. `scientific-slides` for talks. `pptx-posters` for editable PowerPoint posters, `latex-posters` for LaTeX posters, `paper-2-web` for a web or video abstract.
4. Reuse figures from `analysis/`; cite sources on the slide.
5. Render and inspect every slide for overflow and legibility before delivering.
6. Speaker notes: the key sentence and transition per slide.
