### Investigation

**Read-only. Allowed in any state.** A question about what the data, a method, or the literature shows: how does X work, why was Y done this way, are we sure about Z. The deliverable is a cited answer, not new analysis.

1. Restate the question and what would count as an answer.
2. Gather evidence from files and sources you read: `data/` metadata, `analysis/` outputs, `docs/literature-review/`, papers via `paper-lookup`. Use `swarm` when slices are independent.
3. Separate observation, interpretation, and uncertainty. Mark each claim as read, computed, or inferred.
4. If a fact could be settled by a small run, say so and offer the Prototype playbook instead of guessing.

**Reply:** the answer first, then evidence with paths or DOIs, then what remains uncertain.
